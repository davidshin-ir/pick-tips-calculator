Methodology and references
Original MELD formula

MELD = 9.57 × ln(creatinine) + 3.78 × ln(bilirubin) + 11.20 × ln(INR) + 6.43

For MELD only: bilirubin and INR are floored at 1.0; creatinine is restricted to 1.0–4.0 mg/dL and set to 4.0 with qualifying dialysis. Final integer score is restricted to 6–40. This is original/conventional MELD, not MELD-Na.

MELD 3.0 formula

1.33 × (female) + 4.56 × ln(bilirubin) + 0.82 × (137 − sodium) − 0.24 × (137 − sodium) × ln(bilirubin) + 9.09 × ln(INR) + 11.14 × ln(creatinine) + 1.85 × (3.5 − albumin) − 1.83 × (3.5 − albumin) × ln(creatinine) + 6

Bilirubin and INR are floored at 1.0; creatinine is restricted to 1.0–3.0 mg/dL (3.0 with qualifying dialysis); sodium 125–137 mEq/L; albumin 1.5–3.5 g/dL. Score is rounded to the nearest integer and restricted to 6–40. The female indicator follows the published equation.

FIPS formula and interpretation

FIPS = 1.43 × log10(bilirubin) − 1.71/creatinine + 0.02 × age − 0.02 × albumin (g/L) + 0.81

Albumin entered in g/dL is multiplied by 10 to convert to g/L. FIPS uses measured bilirubin, creatinine, and albumin, not MELD-adjusted inputs. FIPS ≥0.92: higher-risk category in elective-TIPS cohorts. Dialysis status does not alter the FIPS formula.
